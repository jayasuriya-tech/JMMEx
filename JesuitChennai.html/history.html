Jesuit Migrants Ministry External (JMMEx)

Empowering and supporting overseas migrant workers through strategic intervention, skill development, and entrepreneurship.

Our Genesis & History

The seed of thought of starting an apostolate exclusively for migrants started on 10th August 2015 in Chennai. Fr. Devadass SJ, the then provincial of The Jesuit Madurai Province, took the initiative, and Fr. Jebamalai Raja SJ, the then Superior of The Jesuit Chennai Mission, joined him in thought. Fr. Sebasti L. Raj SJ, Provincial of The Jesuit Madurai Province, officially established the work by providing the necessary personnel and finances.

The initial explorations and extensive research were conducted by Fr. Arockiasamy SJ (2 years) and Fr. Jayaseelan SJ (1 year). Fr. Eugine Sebastian Rosario SJ served as the first founding director of the Jesuit Migrants Ministry (JMM). He initially began exploring structural possibilities with a localized team within Chennai.

Strategic Shift & Structural Evolution

Based on extensive fieldwork and research, the ministry recognized the critical need to identify the root causes of systemic challenges faced by overseas migrant workers. Having analyzed these hardships, Fr. Eugine SJ felt the urgent need to empower workers with entrepreneurial capabilities and proposed shifting the JMM base from Chennai to Kasthambadi.

Data from the 2015 Tamil Nadu Migration Survey (TMS) highlighted that the Thiruvannamalai district had a high density of undocumented migrant workers. Consequently, from 2017 to 2019, under the leadership of Fr. Eugine Muthu SJ (Director) and Fr. Prabu SJ (Assistant Director), the JMM office was established at the Loyola College Campus in Vettavalam to streamline operations across the district.

05 February 2019 Maiden Self-Help Group launched with 15 founding members to instill savings habits.
6 Core Categories Structured services classified to systematically address and resolve key migrant challenges.
800+ Families Actively supported across 350 villages in the region today.

Present Operations & Expansion

In late 2019, Fr. Prabu SJ stepped into the role of Director, accompanied by Fr. Velankanni SJ as Assistant Director. Following a comprehensive requirements analysis, the headquarters successfully transitioned to Kasthambadi. To match this refined focus, the organization rebranded as the Jesuit Migrants Ministry External (JMMEx), introducing a sharpened logo depicting the specific trades migrants engage in abroad.

In 2021, Fr. Velankanni assumed leadership as Director, supported by Fr. Prabu. Today, the expanded footprint of JMMEx systematically caters to over 800 migrant families spread across approximately 350 villages spanning the districts of Thiruvannamalai, Kallakurichi, Cuddalore, and Viluppuram.
